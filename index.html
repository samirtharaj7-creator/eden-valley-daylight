Morning · Who we are

Education that heals.

For more than 60 years, Eden Valley has offered natural health education. Our 14-day live-in programs help guests adopt lifestyles that address diabetes, cancer, obesity, and heart disease.

“Our mission is to see lives transformed by God’s principles of education and healing.”

Golden hour · Our story

Rooted here since 1962.

  • 1961

    Harold & Effie Grosboll and Pete & Ann Borris set out to start a self-supporting ministry.

  • 1962

    Eden Valley is incorporated in a valley west of Loveland already called “Eden Valley.”

  • 1982

    The lifestyle center opens for people in health crisis.

  • Today

    Wellness, farm, missionary school and market — with ministries in many countries.
