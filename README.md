# Eden Valley — Daylight homepage concept

A homepage design concept for [Eden Valley Institute of Wellness](https://www.eden-valley.org) in Loveland, Colorado.

As you scroll, the sun crosses the sky above a real aerial photo of the valley, and the page moves through the day: dawn, morning, midday, afternoon, golden hour, dusk and night. Each time of day holds one part of the homepage (about, programs, your 14-day stay, story and events, giving, newsletter and contact).

## Viewing it

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is a single static page with no build step.

## Notes

- Photos load from Eden Valley's own website. For a live site, use the original files from Eden Valley.
- Prices, dates, history and program details come from eden-valley.org as of November 2026.
- Buttons and forms (apply, donate, newsletter) are not connected yet.
